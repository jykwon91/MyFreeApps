MyGamingAssistant

A public library of Valorant and CS2 utility lineups: where to stand, where to aim, and where it lands, shown on the minimap with the video clip.

The lineups come from YouTube tutorials. A computer-vision pipeline reads the video frames and the in-game minimap to find the stand and landing positions, then publishes the clips to Cloudflare R2.

The MyFreeApps platform

Since April 2026 I've designed, built, and operated MyFreeApps: four apps sharing one platform, one server, and one CI/CD pipeline. FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and SQLAlchemy on the backend; React, TypeScript, and Redux Toolkit on the frontend; Docker and Caddy in production. Auth, RBAC, TOTP 2FA, account lockout, encrypted PII, audit logging, breached-password checks, and CAPTCHA are written once in a shared package and used by every app. Deploy workflows are generated from one template, with tests that fail CI if an app drifts from it.
